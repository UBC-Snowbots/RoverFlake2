# Refreshment Delivery

Transport the snack box through **7 gates in order** (GPS coordinates provided) over progressively harder terrain.

## Gates
- Marked by **2 poles with red flags**, ≥60 cm tall, **1.5 m apart**
- The **entire rover** must pass through to score

## Cargo
- Box 20 × 9 × 6 cm, <1 kg, mounted **top facing up**
- **Vibration sensor trips at 10 G** (any axis) — drive smooth
- **Bonus:** finish without tripping the sensor

## Terrain
Steep inclines, rocky ground, vertical drops up to **1 m**, bridges, unstable surfaces.
